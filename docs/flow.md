# Flow

lead in → extract → score → reply → sheet → human routing

## Scores
- emergency: no cooling, leak, burning smell, needs someone tonight → AI replies + call/SMS owner
- normal: install, maintenance, quote, out of area, "how much for an AC?" → AI replies + notify staff
- spam: bots / promo / fake number → log only, no reply

## Rules
- Do not ignore humans. Price questions are normal.
- We do not sell AC units off the shelf. Ask for ZIP + repair or install.
- AI is not a technician. No diagnosis. No dollar quote.
- If owner misses the call (30–45s): SMS owner + call staff.
